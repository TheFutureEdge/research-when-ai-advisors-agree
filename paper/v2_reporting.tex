\section{Inference and reporting details}

Sector/class-block intervals are [3.95, 16.14] points for the mean-sign high-minus-lower accuracy gap and [$-5.92$, 5.93] for the modal-direction gap. The excess-error Q4--Q1 interval is [$-2.32$, 0.95] pp. All 5,000 block draws have nonempty comparison groups. These are a dependence sensitivity, not a correction that makes four common market periods independent.

For the original log-error and excess-error regressions, residual standard errors are 0.889 log units and 5.088 pp. Partial $R^2$ for disagreement is 0.000022 and 0.000134, conditional on the specified other covariates; neither is a causal importance measure. In the raw-error sensitivity the standardized disagreement coefficient is +0.219 pp [$-1.786$, 2.500], with $R^2=0.160$ and residual standard error 16.974 pp. Coefficient intervals remain conditional on observed periods.

Council-minus-no-change RMSE is +1.135, +0.943, +0.598, and +0.511 pp across the four disagreement quartiles. The Q4--Q1 contrast interval is [$-2.05$, 1.04] pp, computed from paired resamples of squared errors followed by square roots. The lowest-quartile difference has an interval [0.061, 1.977] pp, reinforcing that small raw loss in this subset is not the same as beating its baseline under every loss.

The companion's analysis inventory enumerates executed comparisons. V2 reports 16 sign-band/cutoff combinations, each with higher/lower agreement groups, and 80 selector/coverage combinations. They complement the original cohort, spread, aggregation, and risk-window sensitivities; they are not independent replications or a multiplicity-controlled confirmatory family. The study developed through exploratory drafting from one frozen outcome vintage. A prospective specification log was not maintained, so the inventory should not be interpreted as proof that no other exploratory choice was considered.
