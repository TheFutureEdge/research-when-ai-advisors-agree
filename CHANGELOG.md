# Version 2.0.0 - 14 October 2026

This revision retains the 8 October outcome cutoff, 232 primary assets, 928 outcomes, and the original historical configuration set. It adds no forecasts or provider-model comparisons. Version 1 remains available through Git history, its release tag, and the original Zenodo record.

- Direction is now evaluated using both the sign of the arithmetic mean and the modal framework vote. Modal ties fall back to mean sign; a no-ties sensitivity is also reported. The positive agreement contrast is specific to mean-sign accuracy and does not survive modal voting.
- Exact baseline-relative magnitude comparisons replace visual inference from approximate chart markers. No resolved excess-error advantage is claimed for strong agreement.
- A second whole-asset bootstrap recomputes rank/selection membership. It targets a resampled asset population conditional on the four observed periods; it does not resolve arbitrary common-market dependence.
- Added period-specific and leave-one-batch-out contrasts, risk/momentum strata, equity/expanded-cohort checks, sign-band/cutoff grids, agreement levels, and confusion matrices.
- Added larger sector/class resampling blocks, raw-error regression and RMSE-relative sensitivities, and an explicit executed-analysis inventory. The volatility selector advantage is almost absent after omitting Batch 3, which now qualifies the pooled comparison.
- Added scale-aware error and pooled baseline-skill diagnostics, dense retention curves, residualized spread, unfitted comparator and per-framework results, forecast-scale summaries, dependence matrices, mode covariance diagnostics, and signed-momentum regression sensitivity.
- Added cohort attrition summaries, class composition, entry delays, window-overlap and regression diagnostics; practical effect-size bounds are explicitly retrospective, with no equivalence claim.
- Added a compatible market-summary adapter, synthetic checks, and reconstruction recipe. The licensed outcome panel and daily price history remain private. Full outcome reproduction is not self-contained in this public edition.
- An additional reconstruction check identified two Batch 3 commodity records with different forecast and historical outcome anchor bases. The primary entry-based scores are preserved. Both entry and original-anchor sensitivities excluding those records are reported; neither resolves the excess-error gradient.
- Updated the abstract, narrative, relevant theory, eleven vector figures, computed appendix, methodology, tested environment, and versioned citations. Approved commercial-interest and AI-assistance disclosures are retained.

## What this revision cannot establish

Four overlapping periods are insufficient to validate future-regime performance. Author-side reconstruction is not independent external market-data replication. Historical shared-backbone framework diversity is not provider diversity or causal independence. Neither unresolved intervals nor an exploratory favorable result establishes calibration, equivalence, economic value, or deployment readiness.
